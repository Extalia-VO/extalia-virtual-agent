# Third-party software notices

Extalia's graph exploration and star styling adapt selected MIT-licensed
navigation and rendering math from
[Advanced Graph View](https://github.com/n23eos/advanced_graph_view), pinned at
`17e3eb1fd0e64d4d13d9a130c4bd77b2f287b48d`.

The full upstream copyright and license are in
[THIRD_PARTY_SOFTWARE.md](apps/web/public/THIRD_PARTY_SOFTWARE.md), which ships
alongside the Web and Desktop renderer. No Obsidian plugin or Pixi runtime is
included. Scene asset licenses are documented separately in
[the asset notices](apps/web/public-assets/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).


The bundled anti-slop instruction family adapts MIT-licensed text from
[anti-slop](https://github.com/miqdadbadjuber/anti-slop), pinned at
`41b85ede0257f39bfcffedf3f58c3c59bcc8530d` (3.2.28). The complete notice ships
in each instruction body and in the Web/Desktop notices linked above. Source
hashes and adaptation boundaries are recorded in
[the provenance manifest](packages/host/src/builtin/antislop-provenance.json).


The Superpowers workflow adaptations use MIT-licensed instructions from
[obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers), pinned at
`22f0b463e7a5e579904dd6211a520321035526f7` (7.0.2). The complete notice ships
inside each instruction body and the Web/Desktop notices.
[Source pins](packages/host/src/builtin/superpowers-provenance.json).

Two OpenDesign brief/reference adaptations use Apache-2.0 materials from
[nexu-io/open-design](https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design), pinned at
`3d8bc24158ae858c8c3842c92118b5bb0bbf6af7`. The full upstream license ships
inside the `open-design` body and Web/Desktop notices; modifications are noted
in both procedures. No separately licensed catalog skills or templates are
included. [Source pins](packages/host/src/builtin/open-design-provenance.json).
